## Основа для преподавателя

Оба урока рассчитаны примерно на 30 минут индивидуального занятия. Lesson 2 опирается на Lesson 1. Основная база: Present/Past Simple, вопросы и отрицания с do/did, can, want/need, простые причины с because. В связных текстах также встречаются Present Continuous, be going to, Present Perfect и знакомые сочетания с -ing/to. Их понимание нельзя считать проверенным по одному обозначению B1: полный маршрут конкретного ученика не предоставлен. Отдельно преподавать First Conditional, Third Conditional, Passive или Reported Speech в этих уроках не планируется. В начальной беседе проверить, нужна ли краткая поддержка Past Simple; при существенном пробеле реальное время увеличится. Если времени не хватает, сократить дополнительное обсуждение после короткой практики и разобрать одну финальную ситуацию с уточнением вместо двух; сохранить самостоятельный итоговый ответ.

Все 12 единиц из карточек сохранены. Prepare в данном уроке — подготовка к событию, revise — повторение изученного к экзамену (британское употребление). Не заменять revise словом review. In your place / in your position / in your situation сохраняются как альтернативы совета, не как три искусственно противопоставленные по значению конструкции.

## B1D1-M07 · Discover the pattern → Rule · 3,5 минуты
Компоненты: Text + Choice → одно окно Rule.
Title: What do these sentences mean?
Subtitle: Look at the examples from Dana’s conversation.
Body:
**If I had enough work for six months, I’d take a chance.**
**I wouldn’t leave just for one project.**
**If I earned more, I would.**

1. Is Dana describing the past or imagining a different situation?
A. Describing what she did last year.
B. Confirming a definite plan for next week.
C. Imagining a different situation now or in the future.
2. In “I’d take a chance”, what does ’d mean?
A. would
B. had
3. Which part introduces the imagined condition?
A. I’d take a chance.
B. If I had enough work for six months.
Key: 1 C; 2 A; 3 B.
После OK → Rule; сохранить один связный блок с абзацами.
Rule title: Imagining a different situation
Rule body:
Use the **second conditional** to explore a situation you are imagining now or in the future. You are not presenting it as a fact or a definite plan. For example, **If I earned more, I’d rent an apartment on my own** imagines having a higher income and a different home.

The pattern is **if + Past Simple, would + verb**. The past form shows an imagined condition; it does not put this conversation in the past. **I’d** means **I would**, and **wouldn’t** means **would not**.

**If we found a suitable apartment, we’d move in together.**
**I wouldn’t take the job if it included every weekend.**

The two parts can change places. A comma normally separates them when the if-part comes first. In this pattern, put **would** in the result, not in the if-part. After **would/wouldn’t**, use the basic verb, without **to** or **-s**.

To ask about a choice, use **Would you + verb … if + Past Simple?** To ask for an idea, use **What would you do if …?**
**Would you go freelance if you had regular clients?**
**What would you do if the company changed the hours?**

For imagined ability, **can** becomes **could**: **If I could choose my hours, I’d start early.** With **be**, **were** is a standard form for an imagined situation: **If the apartment were cheaper, I’d rent it.** You may also hear **was** with I/he/she/it in conversation.
Преподавателю: не вводить всю систему conditionals. Не объяснять Second Conditional как «только невозможное»: здесь обсуждается воображаемый вариант, а не обязательно невозможное событие.

# LESSON 2 · IF I WERE YOU …

Final Goal: понять проблему и ограничения собеседника, дать конкретный совет и скорректировать его после уточнения.
Время: 29 минут основных этапов + 1 минута резерва. План, не фактический хронометраж.

## B1D2-M01 · Opening discussion · 1,5 минуты

## B1D2-M02 · Words and useful combinations · 2,5 минуты
Механика: Matching — слово ↔ определение.
Title: Match the verbs with their meanings.
Subtitle: —
Body:
Слова:
1. prepare
2. revise
3. concentrate
4. avoid
5. consider
6. refuse
Определения — перемешанный порядок:
A. keep your attention on one activity
B. think carefully about an idea before deciding
C. get ready for something
D. say that you will not accept or do something

Key: 1 C; 2 E; 3 A; 4 F; 5 B; 6 D.

**prepare for** an interview
**revise** a topic / **revise for** an exam
**concentrate on** one task
**avoid checking** your phone
**consider joining** a group
**refuse to do** something

Here, **revise** means study again before an exam. It is common in British English; American English often uses **review**.
Преподавателю: concentrate здесь внимание, не «сосредоточиться географически»; consider — обдумать, не принять решение; refuse — явный отказ. Prepare шире revise, поэтому в заданиях не объявлять их взаимоисключающими там, где подходят оба. Эти сочетания вводятся здесь для дальнейших советов, а не как отдельная большая тема gerund/infinitive.

## B1D2-M06 · Notice the advice → Rule · 3,5 минуты
Согласованная правка владельца: 04.10.2026.
Компоненты: Text + Choice → одно окно Rule. Вопросы открываются по одному: OK → стрелка; предыдущие остаются видны, между ними штатные разделители.
Title: How does the writer give advice?
Subtitle: Read the examples and choose the correct answers.
Body:
**If I were you**, **I would** use that free hour to revise one topic.
**In your place**, **I’d** avoid checking messages during that hour.
**In your position**, **I’d** prepare a few questions for the interview.
**If I were you**, **I wouldn’t** accept the job immediately.

1. What does “If I were you” do here?
A. It describes what the speaker did in the past.
B. It imagines being in the other person’s situation to give advice.
C. It promises to do the other person’s work for them.

2. What does “In your position” mean in the third example?
A. I have already experienced the same problem.
B. I want to make this decision for you.
C. I am imagining myself in your situation.

3. What does the speaker mean by “I wouldn’t accept the job immediately”?
A. My advice is to wait before accepting.
B. I predict that you will refuse the job.
C. I am refusing a job that someone has offered me.

4. Compare “I would use”, “I’d prepare” and “I wouldn’t accept”. Which explanation fits these examples?
A. I’d means I had; the next verb describes a past action.
B. I’d means I would; would and wouldn’t are followed by the base verb without to.
C. I’d means I would; would and wouldn’t are followed by to + verb.

Key: 1 B; 2 C; 3 A; 4 B.
ID вопросов: B1D2-M06-discover-v2. Ответы предыдущей версии не переносятся в новый набор.
После четвёртой попытки и стрелки → существующее полное Rule.
Rule title: Giving advice from someone else’s point of view
Rule body:
When someone explains a problem, **If I were you, I’d …** helps you say what you recommend. You imagine being in that person’s situation and suggest an action. It is advice about now or the future, not a story about the past.

**If I were you, I’d prepare a few questions.**
To recommend not doing something, use **If I were you, I wouldn’t …**:
**If I were you, I wouldn’t accept immediately.**

Use **were** in this advice pattern. **I’d = I would**, followed by the basic verb: **I’d prepare**, not **I’d to prepare**.

You can also say **In your place, I’d …**, **In your position, I’d …** or **In your situation, I’d …**. These are alternatives; you do not need to use all three in one conversation. The full form is also possible: **If I were in your situation, I’d …**.

Remember the combinations from the Words section: **concentrate on** a task; **avoid studying** all night; **consider asking** for more time; **refuse to share** answers. Would does not change the pattern after the next verb.

Make your advice specific. **I’d prepare** is very general. **I’d prepare three questions about the working hours** tells the other person what to do. Explain why it fits their situation, and respond when they give you new information.

## B1D2-M10 · Final speaking: make the advice useful · 6 минут
Компоненты: Image + Text + Useful phrases; роли и уточнения — в тексте.

Body:

If I were you, I’d …

In your place / position / situation, I’d …

## B1D1_TRANSCRIPT
Связан с B1D1_DIALOGUE; используется у обоих его плееров.

Dana: I like my job, but I’d love to choose my own hours.
Chris: Would you go freelance if you had a few regular clients?
Dana: Maybe. If I had enough work for six months, I’d take a chance. I wouldn’t leave just for one project.

Dana: I’d need a quieter place. My apartment’s small, and my flatmate takes calls all day.
Chris: Would you rent an apartment on your own?
Dana: If I earned more, I would. For now, it’s too expensive.
Chris: What would you do if a company offered you a full-time job with better hours?

## B1D2_TRANSCRIPT
Связан с B1D2_DIALOGUE.

Leah: I have an interview for a weekend job at a café. My exam’s in three weeks, but the money would help.
Owen: If I were you, I’d ask how many weekends they need you to work.
Leah: I checked. Every weekend for the next month.
Owen: In your position, I wouldn’t agree to that. You need time to revise.
Leah: The interview’s tomorrow. I don’t want to cancel it.
Owen: You don’t have to. I’d prepare a few questions and ask about starting after the exam.
Leah: And if they need someone straight away?
Owen: I’d refuse the job for now. In your situation, the exam would come first.
Leah: That sounds fair. I’ll go to the interview and explain.
