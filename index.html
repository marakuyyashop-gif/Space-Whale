QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Is Space Whale already fully launched?+

Not yet. It is in early access while the teaching workspace, curriculum and live classroom are connected step by step.

Who is the platform for?+

It is designed primarily for independent English teachers and their private students.

Which levels will be included?+

The planned core curriculum covers A1, A2 and B1 with one consistent lesson system.

Do students need to install anything?+

No download is planned. Teacher and student spaces run in a modern web browser.

Can I use my own lessons?+

The teacher library is designed to include both Space Whale materials and personal reusable lessons.
